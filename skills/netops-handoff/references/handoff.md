# Handoff details

A useful draft states:

- Business symptom, impact, affected operation, original vantage and time, and current open or closed status.
- Scoped observations with run-qualified evidence IDs and artifact hashes when available; distinguish direct observations from hypotheses and record relevant counterevidence and limits.
- Operator action and actual, partial, failed, or unknown outcome, owner, and any outstanding reconciliation or rollback responsibility.
- Original-flow verification criterion, check results, stability window, coverage, recurrence, and missing or incomparable checks.
- Remaining budget or access gap, responsible owner if known, and the next permitted action or explicit stop reason.

Do not convert an unknown action into a completed one or a scoped diagnosis into business recovery. Mark unverified provenance and redact sensitive identifiers for the recipient. A draft is not a sent message or a ticket update.
