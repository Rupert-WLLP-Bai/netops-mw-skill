# Verify recovery

Work directly from the supplied original scope, criterion, and check records, whether or not an incident controller exists. Compare the same operation, vantage, identity, target, protocol and address family, proxy, workload, unit, and time scope. Ask for a missing criterion or essential comparison; never treat a ping, action completion, or an unrelated healthy flow as business recovery.

Separate independent timestamped observations from owner claims. Check the owner's agreed stability window, cadence, minimum coverage, maximum gap, freshness, and required count. Failed, stale, missing, incomparable, or insufficient checks and recurrence prevent a recovered conclusion. Report what was observed, the criterion and coverage, gaps, and what an owner should check next. Do not initiate production checks without explicit authorization.

Use [verification details](references/verification.md) for evaluating supplied numeric checks and reporting their limits.
