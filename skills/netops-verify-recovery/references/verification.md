# Recovery verification from supplied records

Record the original flow and owner-agreed criterion, including unit and the stability window, cadence, maximum gap, minimum count and coverage, and freshness. For each check, retain its observed and collection times, source, value, unit, result, artifact reference and hash if supplied, and any difference from the original flow. A hash checks byte consistency; it does not authenticate the observer or establish that the observation occurred.

For a numeric `eq`, `gte`, or `lte` criterion, compare finite values in the exact unit and flag any disagreement between a reported outcome and the calculation. Consider passing independent checks in the agreed inclusive window. A failure in that window, including its end, or a later same-scope failure before closure indicates failure or recurrence. Owner claims provide context but cannot substitute for independent checks. Checks from another operation, scope, episode, or policy are not comparable.

If a numeric coverage rule is agreed, use the interval from each passing observation to the earliest of the next passing observation, that observation plus cadence, or the window end; sum disjoint intervals and divide by the window duration. Check count, freshness of the last pass, and maximum gaps, including the window boundaries. Say explicitly when the data or rule is insufficient to calculate coverage. Do not infer stable recovery from a single passing point unless the agreed rule actually permits it. For unsupported criteria, give a scoped manual assessment instead of claiming automated closure.
